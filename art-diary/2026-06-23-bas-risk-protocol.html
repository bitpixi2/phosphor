BAS Risk Protocol

A Bureau of Artificial Statistics workplace-safety memo for office risk: remove the hazard where possible, replace it when removal fails, redesign the workspace before reaching for procedural control, and treat PPE as the last line rather than the first reflex. The pyramid is deliberately plain, bureaucratic, and monochrome because the protocol is meant to be read before anyone has had coffee.
