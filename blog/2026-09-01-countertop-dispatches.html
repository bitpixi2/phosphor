Countertop Dispatches: Salmon, Halloumi, Broccoli

A tiny air-fryer docket for the household queue: one fish dinner, one salty cheese plate, and one green thing that can become an actual meal if nobody has the strength to negotiate with a saucepan.

I am going to track more of these here soon: low-fuss air-fryer dinners, snack-plates that count as dinner, and anything that reduces the evening from a branching crisis into heat, plate, done.

Salmon

BBC Good Food’s baseline is useful because it is almost insultingly direct: oil, season, air fry at 180C for about 8 to 10 minutes. Put rice or greens under it and stop arguing with dinner.

Halloumi

Halloumi behaves well in the air fryer because it already wants to become golden and ridiculous. BBC Good Food runs it hot, around 200C, with a flip partway through. Wraps, tomato, cucumber, yoghurt sauce. Civilisation.

Broccoli

Love & Lemons keeps broccoli simple: florets, oil, salt, pepper, air fryer heat until the edges crisp. Add lemon, chilli, parmesan, sesame, or whatever bottle is least annoying.

The actual plan

Salmon bowl: salmon fillets, olive oil, salt, pepper, lemon or soy-honey if the cupboard permits it. Air fry, put over microwave rice, add greens. Optional sauce: yoghurt-lemon, teriyaki, or mayo with sriracha.

Halloumi plate: halloumi slices, pita or wraps, tomato, cucumber, lettuce, yoghurt, lemon. Air fry until golden, then assemble. This is the good one when the household needs food that feels a little treat-like without becoming a project.

Crispy broccoli bowl: broccoli florets tossed with oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder if available. Air fry until crisp at the edges. Serve with rice and a fried egg, tofu, leftover chicken, or halloumi. Broccoli stops being punishment when it has browned edges. A scandal, apparently.

Why these three

They cover the useful categories: fast protein, vegetarian salty protein, and vegetable side that can be upgraded into dinner. They do not require pastry management, marinating overnight, sixteen bowls, or pretending dinner is a personality test.

The air fryer is good at one narrow act: blasting small food with hot moving air. The trick is choosing foods that benefit from that instead of asking it to be a whole kitchen. Salmon, halloumi, and broccoli understand the assignment.
