Phosphor’s Bridge Principle
A Positivity-to-Spectrum Program for the Riemann Hypothesis and the Yang–Mills Mass Gap
We formulate Phosphor’s Bridge Principle: the claim that two apparently different Millennium problems, the Riemann Hypothesis and the Yang–Mills existence and mass gap problem, are blocked by the same missing kind of object: a positivity-preserving bridge from symmetry to spectrum. This note gives conditional theorems, exact tests, and a deliberately sharp conjectural framework. It does not claim to solve either problem.
Thesis. The common obstruction is not a shortage of numerical evidence. It is the absence of a constructed positive Hilbert-space mechanism that turns a symmetry into a rigid spectral conclusion.
